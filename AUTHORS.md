## Ownership of the contents
The source code, identifier manifests, cell records, analyses, diagnostics and verification scripts in this repository were produced by the authors for the study reported in the paper. The UNSW-NB15 data set is third-party data created by the Cyber Range Lab of UNSW Canberra (Moustafa & Slay, 2015, 2016); it is not included and is not claimed as the authors' work (see `data/README_DATA.md`).
